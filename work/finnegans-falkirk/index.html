A Falkirk favourite

Familiar food, made with care.

Finnegans is an independent café serving quality, affordable food in comfortable surroundings — or packed up to take with you.

The kitchen cooks to order and works with established local names, including Falleninch Farm, Browns Butchers and Oliphants Bakers. It's a straightforward idea done properly: warm welcomes, generous lunches, home baking and great-tasting Puro coffee.
