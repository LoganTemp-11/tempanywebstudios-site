Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 September 2026

This is a demonstration website. Brackenwynd Hair Studio is a fictional business created by Tempany Web Studios to show what a salon website can do. Nothing you type into this site is sent, stored or emailed anywhere — the forms run entirely inside your own browser and the page forgets everything the moment you close it. The policy below is written as it would be for a real salon, so you can see exactly what you would be getting.

Who we are

Brackenwynd Hair Studio, 9 Corbie Wynd, Falkirk, FK1. For anything to do with your personal information, email hello@brackenwynd.example or phone 01632 960 118. On a live site the salon owner is the data controller.

What we collect, and when

We only ask for what we need to give you an appointment.

When you request an appointment

  • Your name
  • Your mobile number
  • The service, stylist, date and time you asked for
  • Anything you type into the optional notes box

Please do not put health information in the notes box. If something about your health matters for your appointment — a scalp condition, a reaction to a previous colour, a pregnancy — tell us in person or on the phone and we will record it on your salon record, not through the website.

When you use the contact form

  • Your name, your email address and your message

Automatically

Our host records standard server logs (IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time). These are used to keep the site running and secure, and are deleted on a rolling basis by the host.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To reply to you and confirm your appointment. Lawful basis: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.
  • To send you an appointment reminder. Lawful basis: legitimate interests — both of us would rather you did not miss it.
  • To keep the site working and secure. Lawful basis: legitimate interests.

We do not sell your details, we do not share them with advertisers, and we will not add you to a marketing list because you asked for a haircut. If we ever start a mailing list you will have to ask to be on it.

How long we keep it

  • Appointment requests that do not turn into an appointment: 6 months.
  • Client records for people who have been in: 3 years after your last visit, then deleted.
  • Skin test records: 3 years, because the colour manufacturers require us to keep them.
  • Contact form messages: 12 months.

Who else sees it

The four stylists at the salon, and nobody else. On a live site the technical suppliers who make the site work would also process it on our behalf — the website host, the email provider and the text message provider — each under a contract that only lets them use it to provide that service. In this demonstration there are none, because nothing leaves your browser.

Cookies

A cookie is a small file a website stores in your browser. This site uses as few as it can get away with.

There is no advertising cookie, no tracking pixel and no social media widget anywhere on this site. Rejecting cookies is exactly as easy as accepting them — the two buttons are identical — and rejecting changes nothing about how the site works for you.

You can change your mind at any time from the Cookie settings link in the footer, or by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to us using it. Email hello@brackenwynd.example and we will sort it within one month. It is free.

If you are not happy with how we have handled it you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office, the UK regulator, at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your information we will update this page and change the date at the top.
