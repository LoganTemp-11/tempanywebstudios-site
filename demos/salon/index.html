About the salon

A wynd off the High Street, since 2011.

Rhona opened Brackenwynd in a two-chair unit on Corbie Wynd with a second-hand backwash and a hairdryer that only worked on cold. Fifteen years later there are four chairs, four of us, and the same rule we started with: one client, one stylist, start to finish.

We book in hours, not in fifteen-minute slots, because rushing a colour is how colour goes wrong. If you’re in for a full head of highlights you get the chair for as long as it takes, and nobody is hovering behind you waiting for it.

Nobody should have to guess what it costs. That’s why every price is on this page.

Before you come

The things everyone asks.

Do I need a skin test?

If you’ve never had colour with us before, yes — at least 48 hours before your appointment. It takes five minutes, it’s free, and you can pop in any time we’re open. It’s a manufacturer requirement, not us being awkward, and we can’t colour without one.

What happens if I need to cancel?

Give us 24 hours’ notice and there’s no charge at all — a text to 07700 900 118 is fine. Under 24 hours we’ll ask for half the appointment price if we can’t fill the slot, and any deposit you’ve paid stays on your account for next time.

Why do you take a deposit on colour?

Only on colour appointments over two hours, and it’s £20. A three-hour balayage that no-one turns up for is most of a stylist’s afternoon gone. The £20 comes straight off your bill on the day.

Can I bring my kids?

Of course. There’s room for a buggy at the front and a box of books and crayons under the waiting bench. If your little one is having their first haircut, ask for Sophie and mention it when you book — she keeps the keepsake curl envelopes behind the desk.

I don’t know what I want. Is that a problem?

That’s most people. Book a Restyle and we’ll spend the first fifteen minutes talking about your hair, how much time you actually have in the morning, and what will still look right in six weeks. Bring photos if you have them — including ones you hate, they’re just as useful.

Do you do gents and beards?

Yes. Callum does gents cuts, skin fades, clipper work and beard shaping, and he trained in a barbershop before he came to us. Gents cuts are 30 minutes, a cut and beard together is 45.

Where do I park?

There are two free bays on Corbie Wynd itself, and the town-centre multi-storey is a three-minute walk. The High Street buses all stop at the top of the wynd, and it’s about ten minutes on foot from Grahamston station.
