Terms & Conditions

Last updated 8 September 2026

This is a demonstration website. Brackenwynd Hair Studio is a fictional business created by Tempany Web Studios. No appointment made on this site is real, no deposit is ever taken, and no money changes hands. The terms below are written the way a real salon’s would be, so you can see what you would be getting.

1. Appointment requests are requests

Sending the form on our home page tells us what you would like. It is not a confirmed booking. We read every request and text you back to confirm, usually within a couple of hours while we are open, and always the next working morning. Your appointment exists once we have replied. If you have not heard from us by the end of the next working day, phone us on 01632 960 118 — do not assume you are in the diary.

2. Cancelling and changing

  • More than 24 hours’ notice: no charge, no questions. A text to 07700 900 118 is enough.
  • Less than 24 hours’ notice: if we cannot fill the slot we may ask for 50% of the appointment price before we book you in again.
  • Not turning up at all: we will ask for 50% of the appointment price, or keep any deposit you have paid, before your next booking.

We know life happens — childcare falls through, cars do not start, people get ill. Tell us and we will be reasonable. It is the silence that costs us the chair.

3. Running late

If you are up to 15 minutes late we will do our best to fit you in, though we may have to shorten the appointment so the person after you is not held up. More than 15 minutes late and we may have to rebook you, and it will count as a late cancellation. If we are running late, we will tell you as soon as we know, and if we keep you waiting more than 15 minutes you can rebook with no charge at all.

4. Deposits on colour

For colour appointments over two hours we ask for a £20 deposit when we confirm the booking. It comes straight off your bill on the day. If you cancel with more than 24 hours’ notice the deposit moves to your new appointment or is refunded in full, whichever you prefer. A three-hour balayage that nobody turns up for is most of a stylist’s afternoon, which is the whole reason the deposit exists.

5. Skin tests — 48 hours, no exceptions

If you have never had colour with us before, we need to carry out a skin test at least 48 hours before your appointment. It takes five minutes, it is free, and you can pop in any time we are open. We also need a fresh test if it has been more than six months since your last colour with us, or if you have had a reaction to a hair product, a tattoo or henna since your last visit.

This is a requirement from the colour manufacturers and our insurers, not a preference. If you have not had a skin test we cannot colour your hair, and the appointment will count as a late cancellation. Please tell us before your appointment if you are pregnant, have a scalp condition, or have had any reaction to hair dye in the past.

6. Children in the salon

Children are genuinely welcome. There is room for a buggy at the front and a box of books and crayons under the waiting bench. A few practical things:

  • Children under 16 need a parent or guardian in the salon for the whole appointment.
  • We do not colour, bleach or perm the hair of anyone under 16.
  • Children who are not being cut need to stay in the waiting area rather than by the chairs — there are hot tools and open scissors at chair height.
  • First haircuts are 30 minutes with Sophie, and you get the keepsake curl in an envelope.

7. Prices

Every price on this site is what you pay. Where a price says “from”, it varies with how long or how thick your hair is, and with how much product a colour takes. We will tell you the price at the consultation, before anything starts — never at the till afterwards. Prices were last reviewed in September 2026 and we will always tell you before they change. We take cash, card and Apple Pay.

8. If you are not happy

Tell us within seven days and come back in so we can look at it. If something is not right and it is our doing, we will put it right at no charge. We would far rather fix it than have you living with hair you do not like. Colour that has been altered at home afterwards is the one thing we cannot take responsibility for.

9. Gift vouchers

Valid for 12 months from the date of issue, can be used against any service, cannot be exchanged for cash, and cannot be replaced if lost.
