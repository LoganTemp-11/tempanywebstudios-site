What we run

Four classes. Pick whichever suits the day you have had.

Every class is drop-in friendly. If you are not sure where to start, come to Slow Flow and we will look after you.

Slow Flow

60 min · All levels

Gentle, unhurried movement linked to the breath. The one to come to first, and the one most people keep coming back to.

Vinyasa

60 min · Some experience

Warmer and faster, with a bit more strength work. You will sweat, but nobody is checking whether your pose is perfect.

Restore

75 min · All levels

Long, supported holds with bolsters and blankets. Closer to a nap than a workout, and exactly what some weeks call for.

Lunch Express

30 min · All levels

A half hour reset in the middle of the working day. Come in what you are wearing, you will not be a sweaty mess afterwards.

Who is teaching

Hi, I am Nia.

I started practising after a back injury in my thirties, when I was told to "just rest" and it was not helping. Yoga gave me a way back that nothing else did.

I have been teaching for eight years now, and I still think the best class is the one where somebody walks in nervous and leaves having laughed at least once. Classes are capped at twelve so I actually know who is in the room.

You do not need to be flexible. You do not need special clothes. You just need an hour.

Before you come

The things everyone asks.

I have never done yoga before. Is that a problem?

Not even slightly. Roughly a third of people in Slow Flow on any given week are in their first month. Come to that one and tell me at the door, and I will keep an eye on you.

What do I need to bring?

Yourself and something comfortable to move in. Mats, blocks, straps, bolsters and blankets are all here. Bring a bottle of water if you like.

I am not flexible at all.

That is a reason to come, not a reason to stay away. Flexibility is a result of practising, not a requirement for starting.

I have an injury or a health condition.

Tell me before class and I will give you alternatives as we go. If you are unsure whether it is suitable, send me a message and we will work it out.

Can I just turn up?

Yes, though classes cap at twelve and the evening ones do fill. Booking ahead saves a wasted trip.
