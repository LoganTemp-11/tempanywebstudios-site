Most complaints about builders come down to the same three things: vague prices, silent phones and an unfinished last job. Here is exactly how a job with us runs.

Before you send it

How far ahead are you booking?

Kitchens and joinery are currently going in six to eight weeks ahead. Extensions and loft conversions need three to four months, mostly because of warrant approval rather than our diary. Small repair work we can often slot in within a fortnight.

Do I need a building warrant?

Almost all extensions, loft conversions and structural alterations do, and so does anything that changes a load-bearing wall. Straight kitchen replacements and joinery usually do not.

We will tell you at the site visit, and if a warrant is needed we prepare the drawings and submit the application to your local authority on your behalf.

How do you price a job?

Fixed price, written down, broken into stages. We do not work on day rates for planned jobs. The quote lists the materials by manufacturer and specification, so if you compare us against someone else you are comparing like for like. Quotes are free within thirty miles of Dollar.

Do you want a deposit up front?

We never ask for money for labour before we start. On larger jobs there is a materials payment once the order is placed, for kitchen units, steels or windows, and then staged payments as each stage completes. Final payment is due after the snag list is signed off, not before.

Can I stay in the house while you work?

In nearly every case, yes. Kitchens usually mean about a week on a temporary sink and a microwave in the dining room, and we will help you set that up. Extensions are worked from the outside for as long as possible, and the wall only comes through near the end.

Are you insured, and what happens if something goes wrong later?

£5m public liability and £10m employers' liability. Ask and we email the certificates with the quote, along with our waste carrier registration and the guarantee wording.

Our workmanship guarantee runs for ten years and it is a real document, not a line on a website. Timber moves and doors need easing after a winter. That is normal, and we do not charge to come back and sort it.
