Weeks 1 to 3

Groundworks

We strip the topsoil, dig the trenches to the engineer's depths and pour concrete strip foundations. Blockwork then brings the walls up to floor level. The string lines come down once the concrete is in.

Weeks 3 to 5

Drains and slab

Drains are laid and pressure tested while the ground is still open. Hardcore is compacted in layers, insulation goes down and the ground floor slab is poured on top.

Weeks 6 to 7

Timber frame

The frame arrives on two lorries as panels made in a workshop. Ground floor walls first, then the floor deck, then the upper walls. With a crane and five people it stands in four working days.

Weeks 8 to 10

Roof

Trusses, sarking boards, membrane, then slate. Once the roof is on the house is wind and watertight, and the trades can work inside whatever the weather is doing.

Weeks 10 to 16

Walls, windows and doors

A blockwork outer wall goes up around the frame and takes a wet dash render. The windows and the front door are fitted and sealed as the walls reach them.

Weeks 17 to 26

Finishing

Gutters, paths, driveway and turf outside. Inside it is wired, plumbed, plastered, fitted and painted. Then we walk you round with a snagging list before you get the keys.

This model is 6,417 pieces, modelled in Blender: every stud, block and slate is its own object.

Questions

Do I need to own the plot first?

No. Fergus will walk a plot with you before you buy it and tell you what the ground is likely to cost.

Who deals with the building warrant?

Your architect applies for it. We build to the approved drawings and arrange the inspections through to the completion certificate.

How do payments work?

In six stages that match the build on this page. You pay for each stage after you have seen it finished.

Can I do some of the work myself?

Yes. About one client in three takes the wind and watertight shell and finishes the inside themselves.
