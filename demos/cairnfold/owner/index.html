Fergus Dunmore, owner

A joiner by trade. He served his time on roofs and still sets out every plot himself, because a house that starts 20 mm out never gets it back.

Fergus grew up in Cairnfold and started as an apprentice at sixteen. He spent nine years with a large housebuilder before going out on his own in 2004.

He does the first walk of every plot, prices alongside Priya, and is on each site at least once a week. His mobile number is on every quote.

Away from work he coaches the under-12s at Cairnfold Thistle and is slowly rebuilding a 1962 Land Rover.

Call Fergus: 01632 960 417

Three things he will not do

Start without a soil report. Quote a price before the drawings are approved. Cover up any work before you have had the chance to see it.
