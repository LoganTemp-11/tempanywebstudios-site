Twenty-two years, about six houses a year

Cairnfold Construction builds timber-frame houses on private plots within forty minutes of the yard. We stay small on purpose, so the person who prices your house is on your site most weeks.

How it started

Fergus Dunmore left a large housebuilder in 2004 with a van, a chop saw and one customer who wanted an extension. The first full house followed in 2007, on a sloping plot two other builders had turned down.

The yard on Quarry Road came in 2011. That is where the plant is kept and where frame panels are checked before they go to site.

The firm in dates

How we work

One site manager per house, from the first peg to the keys. Photos every Friday. A fixed price once the drawings are approved, and any extra shown to you in writing before the work is done.
