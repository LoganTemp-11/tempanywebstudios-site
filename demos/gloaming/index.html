06 · The pour

Three wiggles and a pull-through.

  1. Tilt the cup.Milk goes in high and thin so it dives under the crema instead of sitting on it.
  2. Drop the jug.Close to the surface, the white starts to float. That's the canvas.
  3. Wiggle, walk back.Side to side while drifting backwards stacks the leaves.
  4. Lift and cut.A thin stream straight through the middle draws the stem. Done.

08 · Questions

Things people ask at the till.

How does ordering ahead work?

Put your drink on the docket, pick a collection time and send it. It's made to land on the pass at that time. You pay when you collect, by card or cash.

Do you do dairy-free?

Oat milk on everything for 30p. It's steamed in its own jug so it never meets the dairy one.

Can I bring the dog?

Yes. Water bowl by the door, biscuits behind the counter if you ask nicely.

Is there wifi and somewhere to work?

Wifi, yes, with the password on the board. Plugs on the long table. We ask laptops to move on at lunch if it's busy.

What about allergens?

Every bake has its allergens written at the counter. The kitchen handles nuts, gluten, dairy and eggs, so we can't promise anything is free from traces.

What if I'm late for my slot?

We hold it for fifteen minutes. After that we remake it fresh when you arrive, no charge, because a cold flat white is nobody's fault but the clock's.
