Privacy, in plain words.

Demonstration site. Gloaming Coffee is not a real business. This page shows the privacy notice a real café would carry. Orders sent from this demo go to Tempany Web Studios (Logan Tempany, sole trader, Dollar, Scotland), who is the data controller for them, and are deleted within 30 days.

What we collect

When you order ahead: the name for the cup, your phone number, your drink, your collection time and any note you add. Nothing else. We do not ask for card details online; you pay at the pass.

Why, and on what basis

To make your order and call you if something runs out. The lawful basis is taking steps at your request to fulfil a contract (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). We never use your number for marketing.

Who handles it

The order form is delivered by Web3Forms, which passes it to our email inbox. Web3Forms processes it on our behalf and does not keep or reuse it beyond delivery. The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes your IP address to serve the page securely.

How long we keep it

Orders are deleted from the inbox within 30 days of collection.

Cookies and storage

The site stores three small things in your browser, none of which track you: your light or dark setting (gl-theme), the drink on your docket (gl-docket) and your cookie choice (gl-consent). These are strictly necessary for features you use, so they need no permission.

Analytics (Google Analytics 4) stays off unless you press "That's fine" on the cookie bar. If you do, Google sets _ga cookies to count visits anonymously, kept for up to 14 months. You can change your mind any time with "Cookie settings" at the foot of the home page.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email logan@tempanywebstudios.co.uk. If you're unhappy with our answer you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
