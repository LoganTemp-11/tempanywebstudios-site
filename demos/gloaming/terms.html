Terms for ordering ahead.

Demonstration site. Gloaming Coffee is not a real business and no drink will be made. These are the terms a real café would publish, written for this one.

Your order

Sending the form asks us to make your drink for the time you picked. It becomes a contract when you collect and pay. If we can't make it (we've run out of oat, the machine's down) we'll call the number you gave before your slot.

Paying

You pay at the pass when you collect, by card or cash, at the prices on the board that day. Prices include VAT.

Collection

We hold an order for fifteen minutes after its slot. After that we'll remake it fresh when you arrive at no charge. If you can't come, call us and we'll cancel it. There is nothing to refund because nothing was taken in advance.

Allergens

Allergen information for every bake is at the counter. The kitchen handles nuts, gluten, milk, eggs and sesame, so we can't guarantee anything is free from traces. Tell us about an allergy when you collect and we'll talk you through it.

Hot drinks

Drinks are served hot. Take care, especially with children nearby.

This website

We keep the menu and hours up to date, but the board in the café is the final word. The text, film and design of this site belong to their owners and may not be copied for commercial use.

The law
