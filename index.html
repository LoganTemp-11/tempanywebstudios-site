The quote

A site that just looks nice isn't doing its job. If a price isn't confident enough to put in writing, something is wrong with the price. So here is the whole thing, built to get people to actually pick up the phone.

01

The price

£399fixed

£200 to start, £199 when you approve it. Nothing else added afterwards unless you ask for it. No VAT, because I'm not registered for it.

Already have a site that just needs fixed?£95 audit

Credited against a rebuild, and sometimes the honest answer is a few changes rather than a new site.

02

The time

10–14days

From a proper brief to the site being live, as long as you're quick to reply along the way. The clock pauses while I'm waiting on content or a decision, and starts again when it arrives. If a job genuinely needs more than five pages, I'll say so before we start, not after.

03

The site

Up to five pages, written from your own words and hand-coded rather than dropped into a theme. Built mobile-first and tested on a real phone, not a browser window stretched narrow. An enquiry form and click-to-call, a Google Business Profile set up properly, and the basic local search groundwork done. Two rounds of changes before it goes live.

Not included unless written into the quote: domain registration, hosting, e-commerce, professional photography and full brand design.

04

What you own

Your domain, your hosting, your code, in your name from day one. Leave whenever you like, for me or for anyone else, and the site keeps working exactly as it did the day before. I don't build anything that only I can maintain, so if I'm ever ill, busy, or stop doing this altogether, you lose nothing, because none of it depends on me.

05

After launch

Small text and photo changes are free for 30 days. After that it's £25 an hour, billed in half-hour steps, nothing rounded up. No monthly fee, no contract, no obligation to ever come back.

06

When five pages isn't the job

Some businesses need the site to do something, not just say something. I build those on the same hand-coded stack, and each one is added on top of the £399 as its own line, not hidden inside it. Jennifer Ellis Yoga is the first one in practice: a booking calendar that holds the slot, and an admin page she updates herself.
