Website quote

Hand-built websites for small businesses that need enquiries, not a brochure.

A site that just looks nice isn't doing its job. Most web design studios also want a call before they'll give you a number, and I think that's backwards too. If a price isn't confident enough to put in writing, something's wrong with the price. So here's the whole thing, built to get people to actually pick up the phone, before you've had to ask.

01

Who's doing this

It's just me. I'm Logan Tempany, and I design, write and build every site myself, in Dollar. No account manager, no handoff to a junior partway through, no agency chain between you and the person doing the work. If something needs changed, you email me directly and I do it.

02

The site

Up to five pages, written from your own words and hand-coded rather than dropped into a theme. Built mobile-first and tested on a real phone, not just a browser window stretched narrow. An enquiry form and click-to-call, a Google Business Profile set up properly, and the basic local search groundwork done. Two rounds of changes before it goes live.

Not included: domain registration, hosting, e-commerce, professional photography and full brand design, unless they're written into the quote. Booking systems, card payment and extra pages are built too, and priced separately in clause 07 rather than buried in the number above.

03

The price

Complete website, up to 5 pages £399

£200 to start, £199 when you approve it. Nothing else added afterwards unless you ask for it.

Already have a site that just needs fixed? £95 audit, credited to a rebuild
04

The time

About ten days from a proper brief to the site being live, as long as you're quick to reply along the way. If a job genuinely needs more than five pages, I'll say so before we start, not after.

05

What you own

Your domain, your hosting, your code, in your name from day one. Leave whenever you like, for me or for anyone else, and the site keeps working exactly as it did the day before. I don't build anything that only I can maintain, so if I'm ever ill, busy, or stop doing this altogether, you lose nothing, because none of it depends on me.

06

After launch

£25 an hour for changes, billed in half-hour steps, nothing rounded up. No monthly fee, no contract, no obligation to ever come back.

07

When five pages isn't the job

Some businesses need the site to do something, not just say something. Taking bookings and holding the slot so two people can't take it at once. Charging a card at the point of booking. A timetable the owner updates themselves without phoning me. I build those, properly, on the same hand-coded stack as everything else, and each one is added on top of the £399 above as its own line, not hidden inside it.

Booking with live availability and an admin page you control from £350
Card payment taken at the point of booking from £120
Each page beyond the first five £45

So a five-page site that takes bookings starts at £749, which is the £399 plus the £350, and everything is quoted before anything starts, the same as the rest of this. If your job doesn't need any of it, don't buy any of it.

08

Questions you might have

What happens after I get in touch?
I look at your business, and your current site if you have one, then reply with an honest recommendation and the real scope, before anything's agreed.
Can you fix my existing site instead of rebuilding it?
Often, yes. That's what the £95 audit is for, and sometimes the honest answer is a few changes rather than a full rebuild.
Is £399 really the price for every project?
For the standard scope above, yes. If your business needs something bigger, I'll say so and give you the real number before any work starts, never after.
