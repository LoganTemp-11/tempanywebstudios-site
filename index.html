The quote

Most studios want a call before they'll give you a number. I think that's backwards. If a price isn't confident enough to put in writing, something is wrong with the price, so here's the whole thing: the price, the time, what you own and the sites to judge it by.

The price

£599fixed

£200 to start, £399 when you approve it. That's the founding price for anyone who books by 28 February 2027; from 1 March 2027 it's £749. Nothing else added afterwards unless you ask for it. No VAT, because I'm not registered for it.

The time

10–14days

From a proper brief to the site being live, as long as you're quick to reply along the way. The clock pauses while I'm waiting on content or a decision, and starts again when it arrives. If a job genuinely needs more than five pages, I'll say so before we start.

The site

Up to five pages, written from your own words and coded by hand. Built for phones first and tested on a real one. An enquiry form and click-to-call, a Google Business Profile set up properly, and the basic local search groundwork done. Two rounds of changes before it goes live.

Two small bills come to you, not me: your domain, about £8 to £19 a year to the registrar, and hosting, which is free for a site this size. Not included unless written into the quote: e-commerce, professional photography and full brand design.

What you own

Your domain, your hosting, your code, in your name from day one. Leave whenever you like, for me or for anyone else, and the site keeps working exactly as it did the day before. I don't build anything that only I can maintain, so if I'm ever ill, busy, or stop doing this altogether, you lose nothing, because none of it depends on me.

After launch

Small text and photo changes are free for 30 days. After that it's £25 an hour, billed in half-hour steps, nothing rounded up. Or there's the care plan at £20 a month: I look after your renewals (the registrar's fee stays yours), check the site every month and make up to an hour of small changes, and you can cancel any time. The one exception is my own booking system, below: that comes with the care plan for its first 12 months, because software that takes your bookings needs someone checking it. Without it there's no monthly fee and no obligation to ever come back.

When five pages isn't the job

Bookings, card payments and extra pages

Some businesses need more than five pages, or a site that takes bookings or payments. I build those on the same hand-coded stack. Bookings come two ways; everything else is its own line on top of the £599.
