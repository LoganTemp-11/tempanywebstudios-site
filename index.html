Straight answers before we start

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What happens after I enquire?

I will review your business, your current website if you have one, and what you want to achieve. I will then reply with an honest recommendation, likely scope and next step.

Do I own the finished website?

Yes. Once the agreed balance is paid, the finished website and its approved content are handed over to you. Your domain and hosting are kept in your name from the start.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. I can assess whether a focused redesign is worthwhile or whether smaller changes would solve the problem more efficiently.

Is £399 the final price for every project?

It is the starting price for a focused founding-client website. I confirm the exact scope and fixed price in writing before any payment is due.

How long will my website take?

A focused founding-client website will usually be ready within 10–14 days once the deposit, content and required access have been received. I will confirm the schedule before the project starts.

What is not included in the starting price?

Domain registration, hosting, paid third-party tools, e-commerce, booking systems, custom databases, professional photography and full brand design are not included unless they are written into your quotation.

Service terms

Website information and prices are indicative until a written scope and fixed quotation are agreed. Founding-client projects start from £399. The standard starting scope is up to five focused pages, mobile-first development, shaped website copy, an enquiry form, basic search setup, launch checks and one organised revision round.

A focused project will usually take 10–14 days after the deposit, agreed content and required account access have been received. This timing depends on prompt client feedback and is confirmed in writing before work begins.

Unless agreed otherwise in writing, 50% is due before work begins and the remaining 50% is due on final approval before launch or handover. Ownership of the final approved website and approved project content transfers after full payment. The client remains responsible for ensuring that supplied text, photographs, logos and other materials may lawfully be used.

Domain registration, hosting, paid licences and third-party services are separate and should be held in the client's own name. E-commerce, booking systems, custom databases, professional photography, full brand design and major scope changes are excluded unless specifically included in the quotation.

Project-specific scope, revisions, responsibilities, cancellation, delays and post-launch support are confirmed in the written proposal or agreement before payment. That project agreement takes priority over this website summary.
