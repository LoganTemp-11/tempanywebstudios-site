Privacy notice

Last updated: 1 September 2026. Logan Tempany, trading as Tempany Web Studios in Dollar, Scotland, is responsible for the personal information collected through this website. Questions can be sent to logan@tempanywebstudios.co.uk.

The reply form collects your name, business name, email address, current website if supplied, what your enquiry is about, and the details you enter in your message. This information is used to review and reply to your enquiry, prepare a quotation and, if you choose to proceed, arrange and deliver the requested work. It is processed to take steps at your request before a possible contract, to manage genuine business enquiries, and to perform any agreement you enter into.

The form uses Web3Forms to deliver submissions to the business inbox without opening your email app. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Web3Forms states that submission data may be retained for a maximum of three years, or for a shorter period where the plan provides one or it is deleted earlier, and may be processed outside the UK under the safeguards described in its policy. Google processes the delivered email as the inbox provider.

Enquiries that do not become projects are normally deleted from the business inbox within 12 months. If work proceeds, relevant communications may be kept for longer where needed to deliver the project, maintain financial records, enforce an agreement or resolve a dispute. Information is not sold or used for unrelated marketing.

This website does not currently use advertising trackers or non-essential cookies. You may ask for access to your personal information, request correction or deletion where applicable, or restrict or object to certain processing. You may also make a data protection complaint using the process below.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies. It keeps one thing in your browser, whether you chose the light or the dark version, and only if you press the switch; nothing is stored until you do. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies, does not fingerprint the device and does not follow you to other sites. That is why there is no cookie banner: a setting you chose yourself is not something to ask consent for, and there is nothing else.

Complaints

Tempany Web Studios will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days of receiving it. Appropriate steps will then be taken to investigate, you will be kept informed where necessary, and the outcome will be communicated without undue delay.

If you remain dissatisfied, you may complain to the UK supervisory authority, the Information Commissioner's Office. You do not have to wait for this internal process if you prefer to contact the ICO directly.
