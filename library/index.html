Two of these are real clients’ sites, live on their own addresses. The other five are complete demonstrations I built to show what a site can do for one kind of business. I’ve ranked them myself, by craft, so you see the best of it first. Every one opens on your phone.

  1. 01

    Demonstration

    Number 1: Gloaming Coffee

    Café · order ahead, collect at the pass

    A film of the shot being pulled plays as you scroll, and the order docket clipped to the edge prints the dose, the shot time and the milk temperature in step with it. Order ahead and the docket gets stamped COLLECT.

    Why it sits here The page is one continuous scene rather than a stack of sections. The most ambitious build in the library.

    Open ittempanywebstudios.co.uk/demos/gloaming/

  2. 02

    Demonstration

    Number 2: Brackenwynd Hair Studio

    Salon · Falkirk · every look priced, every stylist named

    Tap a photo of the hair you want and that service is carried into the booking form, with the stylist named and the price shown before anyone sits down.

    Why it sits here Booking by pointing at the result is how people already choose a hairdresser. The site just stops getting in the way.

    Open ittempanywebstudios.co.uk/demos/salon-v2/

  3. 03

    Demonstration

    Number 3: Rowanbank Physiotherapy

    Clinic · Alloa · session pricing in the open

    Tap where it hurts on the body and the page answers: the likely start, the typical course, and what a first appointment costs.

    Why it sits here It answers the question a patient arrives with before they have to phone and ask it.

    Open ittempanywebstudios.co.uk/demos/clinic/

  4. 04

    Live client site

    Number 4: Jennifer Ellis Yoga

    Yoga · Ayrshire and online · she books and edits it herself

    A working booking system: class dates with the places left, the place held the moment someone books, and an admin page Jennifer runs herself.

    Why it sits here Real classes, real bookings, every week. The highest-working site in the library.

    Open ityogawithjenniferellis.co.uk

  5. 05

    Live client site

    Number 5: Rayney Day Pet Services

    Dog walking and pet home checks · Dollar

    Fiona's own photographs, her services in plain words, and one tap to email or ring her from any page.

    Why it sits here Small, quick and clear, which is exactly what a local service needs to be on a phone.

    Open itrayneydaypets.co.uk

  6. 06

    Demonstration

    Number 6: Whinstone Joinery

    Trades · Dollar · the job drawn to scale

    Each job drawn as an elevation, dimensioned and priced, with the quoted figure running beside the invoiced one.

    Why it sits here It proves a builder's honesty with numbers rather than adjectives.

    Open ittempanywebstudios.co.uk/demos/trades/

  7. 07

    Demonstration

    Number 7: Ochil Ledger

    Accountancy · Tillicoultry · a full fee table

    Written as a chaptered statement, with the full fee table in the open and a running total in the margin as you tick what you need.

    Why it sits here Clear and calm. The quietest of the seven, which suits an accountant and is why it closes the shelf.
