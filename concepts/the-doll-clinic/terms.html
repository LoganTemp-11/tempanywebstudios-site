Terms

Booking

Treatments and courses are booked on That Time. Its cancellation policy, shown on the booking page, applies to every appointment.

Consultations and suitability

Semi-permanent make-up and fillers start with a consultation. Demi can decline or postpone a treatment if it isn't suitable for you on the day. Some treatments need a patch test first.

Reviews and top-ups

Filler clients get a free two-week review. Top-ups are only done if needed. Missed appointments carry a £30 fee for top-ups.

Lash extensions

Classic and hybrid sets are for regular clients. New clients should message Demi before booking.

Training

Please message Demi before booking a course so she can confirm it suits you and arrange your dates.

Prices

Prices on this site match the booking page at the time of writing. If they differ, the booking page price applies.
