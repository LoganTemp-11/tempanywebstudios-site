Questions people ask first

How do I book?

On Demi's booking page, That Time. Every treatment she offers is on it, with live times. You can also WhatsApp her if you want to ask something first.

I'm new. Can I book lash extensions?

Classic and hybrid sets on the booking page are for regular clients. If you're new, message Demi first, or start with a Korean lash lift and tint.

Do I need a consultation for semi-permanent brows or lip blush?

Yes. Book the free 15-minute consultation first. It covers what you want, the downtime and the aftercare, and you can ask anything.

How long do fillers last?

Usually 6 to 12 months, depending on the filler and your own metabolism. Every filler treatment starts with a consultation.

Is there a review after filler?

Yes, a free two-week review. Top-ups are only done if they're needed. Missed appointments carry a £30 fee for top-ups.

Do the courses suit complete beginners?

That's who they're for. They're taught 1-1 by Demi. Message her before booking so she can match you to the right course.
