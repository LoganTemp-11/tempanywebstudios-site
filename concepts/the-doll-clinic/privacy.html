Privacy policy

The Doll Clinic is run by Demi at Blush Boutique, 8 Keith Street, Kincardine FK10 4ND. Contact: WhatsApp 07366 021210.

What this website collects

Nothing directly. The question box and the WhatsApp buttons open WhatsApp on your own device with a message ready to send. Bookings are made on That Time, which has its own privacy policy.

What Demi keeps

Your name, contact details and the messages you send, to arrange your appointment. If a treatment needs health questions to be done safely, those answers are kept privately and used only for your treatments.

Why

To reply to you and book your appointment (steps before a contract), and to treat you safely (your explicit consent for health details).

Cookies and analytics

If you allow it, Google Analytics measures which parts of the site are used. It is off unless you say yes. Your choice is stored in your browser. Change it any time from "Cookie settings" at the foot of the home page.

How long

Client records are kept while you are a client and for as long as the law and insurance require after your last treatment. Analytics data is kept for 14 months.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details by messaging Demi. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
