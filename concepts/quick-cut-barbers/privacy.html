Privacy policy

First version of a website for Quick Cut Barbers, made by Tempany Web Studios. Last updated 6 November 2026.

The short version. This page has no form, sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from Google or any other third party. The text to Stephen is written in your browser and opens in your own Messages app; nothing passes through this site. Your lights choice and your cookie answer are kept in your own browser.

Who runs this page

This is a first version of a website for Quick Cut Barbers (S. Miller & Son, 83a Stirling Street, Alva), made by Tempany Web Studios, a web design business run by Logan Tempany, to show the shop what its site could be. It is not yet the shop's own site. For anything about this page, email logan@tempanywebstudios.co.uk.

To book or ask about a cut, text or call Stephen on 07776 237491.

The text to Stephen

"Text Stephen" builds a message from the choices you make (guard, top, beard trim, and your first name if you type one). It runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you pick or type is stored, and this page sends nothing.

"Open in Messages" hands the words to your own phone's messaging app with Stephen's number filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send there yourself, and the message then goes from your phone to his like any other text. "Copy the text" puts the words on your own clipboard.

Cookies and your cookie choice

This site sets no cookies. Two things are kept in your own browser's storage: your lights choice (light or dark), so the page looks the way you left it, and your answer to the cookie question, so it does not ask again. Neither is ever sent anywhere. You can clear them through your browser settings, or change your answer with the Cookie settings link at the foot of any page.

Choosing Accept switches nothing on, because this first version has no analytics to switch on.

The fonts are stored on this site, so your browser does not contact Google Fonts or anyone else to show them.

Hosting

The page is served by Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare keeps short-lived technical logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, to keep the service running and secure. Its policy is at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Opening a preview of this page may let Tempany Web Studios know it has been viewed. How that works is set out under How visits here are handled.
