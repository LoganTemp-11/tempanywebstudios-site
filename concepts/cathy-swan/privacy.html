Privacy policy

Draft, 14 September 2026

This is a first version, not a live site. The form on the front page is a demonstration: it runs entirely inside your own browser, it is not connected to anything, and nothing you type into it is sent, stored or emailed anywhere. The policy below is written for how the site would work once it is live, so that Cathy can read it and say whether it is right before anything is switched on.

Who this would be

Cathy Swan, a sole trader teaching yoga in Perth. Once the site is live she is the data controller for anything sent through it. Her published contact details are cathyswanyoga@gmail.com and 07990 945 752.

The site itself is built and maintained by Tempany Web Studios, which would be a processor acting on her instructions.

What the form would collect

Only what is needed to answer you.

  • Your name
  • An email address or a mobile number, whichever you give
  • What you are asking about
  • Anything you write in the message box

Nothing else is asked for, and nothing is collected in the background beyond the server logs described below.

Please keep health information out of the form

Health information is a special category under UK GDPR and it deserves more care than a web form gives it. If something about your body matters for a class, a workshop or a one-to-one session, tell Cathy in person, by phone or by email rather than typing it here.

Server logs

The host keeps standard logs: IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. They keep the site running and secure, and the host deletes them on a rolling basis. Nobody reads them unless something breaks.

Why it would be used, and the lawful basis

  • To answer your enquiry. Taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.
  • To keep the site working and secure. Legitimate interests.

Your details would not be sold, not shared with advertisers, and not added to a mailing list because you asked a question. If a newsletter ever exists, you would have to ask to be on it.

How long it would be kept

  • Enquiries that do not turn into anything: 12 months, then deleted.
  • Enquiries that do: kept while you are coming to classes, and deleted afterwards on request.

Cathy has not set these periods yet. They are a sensible starting point and are here to be corrected before launch.

Who else would see it

Cathy. On a live site the suppliers who make the site work would also process it on her behalf, the host and whatever handles the form, each under a contract that only lets them use it to provide that service. Today there are none, because nothing leaves your browser.

Cookies and what this page stores

A cookie is a small file a website keeps in your browser. This page uses none at all. It does keep two small values in your browser's own local storage, which never leave your device and are never sent anywhere.

You can change your mind from the cookie bar at any time, or by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask for a copy of what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, ask for it to be deleted, or object to it being used. Email cathyswanyoga@gmail.com and it is sorted within a month. It is free.

If you are not happy with how it was handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes

If this changes, the page changes and so does the date at the top.
