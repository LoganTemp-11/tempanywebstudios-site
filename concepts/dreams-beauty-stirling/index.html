Questions

Can I just walk in?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome, Tuesday to Saturday, 10am to 5pm.

When are you open?

Tuesday to Saturday, 10am to 5pm. Closed Sunday and Monday.

Where are you?

39 Murray Place, Stirling FK8 1DQ, in the city centre, near McDonald's. Open in Google Maps.

My card says Pitt Terrace.

Older loyalty cards and listings show 1B Pitt Terrace. Dreams Beauty is at 39 Murray Place.

How do I book?

Call 01786 357717, or message them on Facebook or Instagram. Walk-ins are fitted in where they can be. Best way to book: to confirm with the salon

What do you do?

Brows (threading, tinting, lamination, henna), lashes, waxing, gel and acrylic nails, manicures, pedicures, makeup, facials and massage.

Do you do brows?

Yes: threading, tinting, lamination and henna brows.

Are lashes back?

Ask in the shop or call first. Semi-permanent lashes were paused from 1 October

How much is a gel manicure?

The offer card says £15 for gel polish on hands; check it is current. Undated card: confirm in the shop

How can I pay?

Card, debit and contactless (Google listing).

Is there a loyalty card?

Yes. Ask in the shop what it gives you. To confirm with the salon
