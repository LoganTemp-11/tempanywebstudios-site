Privacy policy

When this page is opened

This is a preview page under tempanywebstudios.co.uk/concepts/. When it is opened, Logan receives a short notification on his phone, so that he can follow up while the preview is fresh and does not chase anyone who has not seen it.

The notification says which page was opened and when; the approximate town, region and country, and the name of the internet provider, which Cloudflare works out from the connection's IP address; the type of device and browser; and the website the link was followed from, where the browser passes that on. On mobile data the town is often the network's nearest hub rather than where you are. The IP address itself is not included and is not stored. So that one visit sends one notification, a one-way code made from the IP address, the browser and the page is held in Cloudflare's cache for up to 30 minutes and then discarded. Nothing is stored on your device.

Notifications are delivered through Telegram. Logan keeps them for no longer than one month and uses them only to decide when to follow up. To stop notifications about your visits from a browser, open tempanywebstudios.co.uk/quiet/ in it. The full notice is at tempanywebstudios.co.uk/privacy.

The note form

While this is a first version, the note form is not connected to anything. Nothing you type into it is sent or stored: it stays on the page and goes when you close it. To reach the salon, call 01786 357717 or walk in.

Once the salon says yes to the site, the form will send what you type (your name, a phone number or email address, the topic and your note) to Web3Forms, a form service that turns it into an email to the salon, under its own privacy policy. This page will say so when that happens.

"Walk me there"

The street plan and the five walks are drawn into the page itself. Choosing where you are standing runs entirely in your browser: nothing is stored or sent, no request is made, and the page never asks for your location.

Cookies and your cookie choice

This site sets no cookies. Two things are kept in your own browser's storage: your lights choice (light or dark), so the page looks the way you left it, and your answer to the cookie question, so it does not ask again. Neither is ever sent anywhere. You can clear them through your browser settings, or change your answer with the Cookie settings link at the foot of any page.

Choosing Accept switches nothing on, because this first version has no analytics to switch on.

The fonts, photographs and street plan are stored on this site, so your browser does not contact Google Fonts, a map service or anyone else to show them.

Hosting

The page is served by Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare keeps short-lived technical logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, to keep the service running and secure; it also runs the preview notification above. Its policy is at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Links to other sites

The Facebook, Instagram, Google Maps, OpenStreetMap and Telegram links take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.
