Privacy policy

First version of a website for Dreams Beauty, made by Tempany Web Studios. Last updated 6 November 2026.

The short version. This page loads nothing from Google or any other third party, sets no cookies and runs no analytics. If you send a note with the form, it goes by email through Web3Forms. Your lights choice and your cookie answer are kept in your own browser.

Who runs this page

This is a first version of a website for Dreams Beauty, made by Tempany Web Studios, a web design business run by Logan Tempany, to show the salon what its site could be. It is not yet the salon's own site. For anything about this page, email logan@tempanywebstudios.co.uk.

For a booking or anything about a treatment, call the salon on 01786 357717.

The note form

If you use "Leave a note", the form sends what you type (your name, a phone number or email address, the topic and your note) to Web3Forms, a form service that turns it into an email. Web3Forms processes it under its own privacy policy. Nothing you type is stored on this site.

While this is a first version, the form is not yet connected to the salon's inbox. Calling is the quickest way to reach them.

"Walk me there"

The street plan and the five walks are drawn into the page itself. Choosing where you are standing runs entirely in your browser: nothing is stored or sent, no request is made, and the page never asks for your location.

Cookies and your cookie choice

This site sets no cookies. Two things are kept in your own browser's storage: your lights choice (light or dark), so the page looks the way you left it, and your answer to the cookie question, so it does not ask again. Neither is ever sent anywhere. You can clear them through your browser settings, or change your answer with the Cookie settings link at the foot of any page.

Choosing Accept switches nothing on, because this first version has no analytics to switch on.

The fonts, photographs and street plan are stored on this site, so your browser does not contact Google Fonts, a map service or anyone else to show them.

Hosting

The page is served by Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare keeps short-lived technical logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, to keep the service running and secure. Its policy is at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Links to other sites

The Facebook, Instagram, Google Maps and OpenStreetMap links take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal information is held about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. Email logan@tempanywebstudios.co.uk. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If anything here changes, the date at the top changes with it.
