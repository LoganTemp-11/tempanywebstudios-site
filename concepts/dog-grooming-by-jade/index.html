Questions

Before you book.

How much does a groom cost?

It depends on your dog's size and coat. Call or message Jade with the breed and she'll give you a price before you book.

Is Jade qualified and insured?

Yes. Jade holds an iPet Level 3 Diploma in Professional Dog Grooming and an HND in Animal Care, is canine first aid qualified and fully insured.

Can my puppy come for a first groom?

Yes, first grooms are welcome. Tell Jade it's their first visit when you book so she can plan around them.

My dog's coat is matted. Can Jade help?

Tell Jade when you book. She'll look at the coat and talk you through the safest option for your dog.

Where do I drop off?

3A Forrest Road, Stirling FK8 1UH. There is accessible parking.

When is Jade open?

Tuesday to Thursday 9am to 6pm, Friday 9am to 5pm and Saturday 9am to 4pm.
