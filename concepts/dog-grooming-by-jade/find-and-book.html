Questions

Can I bring my dog in just for nails?

Yes. In Stacey’s words, “from full grooms to pop in nail trims”.

Does Jade groom puppies?

Yes. She posted Ruby the mini schnauzer’s first groom on 6 November 2026.

Can I tell Jade how I want my dog to look?

Yes. Amanda says “she listened to our requests, and she did exactly what we asked”, and Daniel says “She listens to what the client wants”.

What will it cost?

Her 2027 list goes on the Grooms page when it’s ready. Her prices change from 5 January 2027, and anything booked for 2026 stays at today’s price. Call or WhatsApp her to ask about your dog.

Is Jade insured and qualified?

Yes. She is fully insured and has been grooming for 4+ years. Her qualifications are HND Animal Care, iPET Level 3 and canine first aid.
