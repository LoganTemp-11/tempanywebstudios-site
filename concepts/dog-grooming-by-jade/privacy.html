Cookies

This site keeps one thing in your browser: your answer to the cookie question, saved as dgj-consent. On this concept, nothing loads whichever you choose, and your answer does not change the notification described below. On Jade’s live site, visits would be counted only after you press Accept.

The request form

On this concept the form is not switched on, so nothing you type is sent. When it is switched on, Web3Forms passes what you send to Jade’s email so she can reply. How long requests are kept: Jade to confirm.
