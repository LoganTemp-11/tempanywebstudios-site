Questions

Can I just walk in?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome Monday to Saturday, 9am to 6pm.

How do I book a time?

Call 07726 776666, or message them on Facebook or Instagram (@dunblanenails_uk).

When are you open?

Monday to Saturday, 9am to 6pm. Closed on Sunday.

How much is a set?

Prices are not online yet. Ask when you call or at the desk. Price list to come from the salon

What do you do?

Acrylic nails, dip powder, manicures (men's too), manicure and pedicure, spa and waterless pedicures, nail art, nail decals, nail polish and polish changes.

Do you do men's nails?

Yes, men's manicures.

Is there a student discount?

Yes, 10% off for students.

Is there a loyalty card?

Yes. Six stamps, and your sixth visit comes with a discount; ask at the desk how much. Amount to confirm with the salon

Where are you?

15 High Street, Dunblane FK15 0EE. Open in Google Maps.

Anything while I wait?

Their Google listing mentions drinks and free wi-fi.

How do I pay?

Ask when you call. To confirm with the salon
