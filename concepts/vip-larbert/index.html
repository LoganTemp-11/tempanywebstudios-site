    Before you come in

    Questions

    Do I need to book?

    No. Walk in during opening hours. Some days there's a short wait, so ring 01324 227530 first if you're tight for time.

    Are you open on Sundays?

    Yes, 10am to 5pm. Monday to Saturday it's 9am to 6pm.

    What's in a Turkish shave?

    A hot towel to soften the skin and beard, then a shave with a straight blade. A full sitting can also include waxing and a quick flame to tidy the ears.

    What's the flame?

    Ear singeing: a small flame used to tidy stray hairs on the ears. It's part of the full Turkish finish. Ask your barber about it in the chair.

    Do you cut kids' hair?

    Yes. Google lists the shop as good for kids, and parents in the reviews say the barbers are excellent with them.

    Is the shop wheelchair accessible?

    Yes. The entrance and car park are wheelchair accessible.

    How much is a cut?

    The full price list is on the board in the shop window. Ring 01324 227530 if you want a price before you come in.
