Why an appointment card, not a photo banner

Most salon sites open on a big photo, a slogan and a Book now button. Yours opens on the thing every client leaves with: the card. Someone ticks what they are in for, the card fills itself in by hand, and they read what one of your own reviewers said about that treatment before they ask for a date. The treatments listed are the ones your Google reviewers mention most, so it already sounds like Lime Salon Group.
