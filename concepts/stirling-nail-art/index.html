Questions

Can I just walk in?

Walk-in appointments are available. Booking on Fresha holds a time.

How do I book?

On Fresha, or call, text or WhatsApp 07347 199692.

When are you open?

Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday and Saturday 10am to 6pm; Thursday 12pm to 8pm; closed Sunday and Monday.

Where are you?

21 Upper Craigs, Stirling FK8 2DG, in the city centre, near public transport. Open in Google Maps.

What is BIAB?

A builder gel layer under the gel colour. It is on the menu with gel polish at £40 (1h 30m), or £30 on the junior menu. Reviews often mention how long it lasts.

Is there a cheaper option?

Yes, the junior menu with Yuliia: a manicure from £15.

Can you take my old gel off?

Yes. Gel removal is £5 and takes 15 minutes.

Do you do pedicures?

Yes, five pedicures from £25, and a gel mani-pedi for £65.

Can I bring my dog or my kids?

Their Fresha page lists the salon as pet-friendly and kid-friendly.

Anything while I wait?

Free tea, coffee and Wi-Fi.

How do I pay?

Card, debit or contactless.

Is there a student discount?

A Tuesday student offer was posted on their Facebook. Ask in salon. Seen on Facebook, to confirm with the salon
