Hello, I'm Karen

Every detail is designed around you

I'm an HND qualified complementary therapist, fully insured, with 14 years in salons and spas behind me. Those years gave me a love of combining massage styles into treatments that don't exist on any menu.

Every appointment starts with a proper chat. We talk through what's sore, which smells you like and how much pressure you want, and you get to smell the oils before we decide on a blend. You decide how long you'd like. I design everything else.

Questions people ask first

What happens at the start?

A consultation. We go through any aches, what pressure you like and which scents you love, and you smell the oils before we blend them. That's why there's an extra 30 minutes on top of your treatment time.

I've never had a massage. Which length should I pick?

60 or 90 minutes is a good start. Tell Karen what you're hoping for and she'll suggest what fits.

Can I have a massage while pregnant?

Pregnancy massage is done on your side with a U-shaped pillow. Mention how far along you are when you book.

Do you do mobile or home visits?

Not any more. All treatments are at The Salon in Tillicoultry.
