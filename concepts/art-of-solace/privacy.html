Privacy policy

Art of Solace is run by Karen Haggerty at The Salon, 33 High Street, Tillicoultry FK13 6AA. Contact: artofsolace@hotmail.com, 07801 284269.

What we collect

When you build a treatment on this site, your choices are written into an email or a message on your own device. Nothing is stored by the website. Once you send it, Karen keeps the message to arrange your appointment.

Consultation notes

Before your treatment Karen asks about aches, health and pregnancy so the treatment is safe for you. These notes are kept privately and used only for your treatments.

Why

To reply to you, book your appointment and treat you safely (steps before a contract, and your explicit consent for health details).

Analytics

If you allow it, Google Analytics measures which parts of the site are used. It is off unless you say yes, and you can change your choice from "Cookie settings" at the foot of the home page.

How long

Messages and consultation notes are kept while you are a client and deleted on request. Analytics data is kept for 14 months.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details by emailing Karen. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
