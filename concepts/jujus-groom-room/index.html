Questions people ask first

What does it cost?

£599, fixed. £200 to start and £399 when you are happy with it. No hosting bill on top.

Is the booking diary extra?

Not in November. It goes in for the same £599 with no monthly fee. The deal ends on 30 November.

Do I lose control of my diary?

No. You choose which slots show as free each week, and nothing is booked until you confirm it. If you would rather keep some regulars on the phone, you can.

What about new clients while I'm full?

They see that the books are closed and can join a waiting list, so you stop getting the same message over and over.

What happens to my Facebook page?

Nothing. It stays as it is, and the site links to it. People who are not on Facebook can see your dogs and your prices too.

What do you need from me?

Your days and drop-off times, your prices, ten or so photos, and a line or two about you.

Want this to be the real one?

Give me a ring or drop me an email. Ten minutes on the phone is enough to get going.
