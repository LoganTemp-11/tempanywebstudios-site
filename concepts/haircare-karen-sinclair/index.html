Why a swatch ring, not a photo banner

Most salon sites open on one big photo, a slogan and a Book now button. Yours opens on the thing a colourist reaches for: the swatch ring. Every strand on it is coloured from your own photos, and picking one turns it to the top and puts that head in the mirror from your logo. It says what your Facebook bio says, colour specialist, without a single line of sales copy.

What I have used, all public

  • Your logo, five photos of your work and your bio, from your Facebook page.
  • 112 Stirling Street, Alva, and 01259 760267, as your page gives them.
  • The postcode FK12 5EH, from your Fresha directory listing.
  • Right now anyone without Facebook, or not logged in, hits a sign-in wall before they see your work or your prices. A site of your own shows both to everyone, and to Google.

What I would still need from you

  • Your opening hours and the prices you want shown.
  • What each photo is, in your words. The names under the strands are my description.
  • Ten or more photos. Off your phone is fine.
  • A line or two about you and the salon.
