Walk in, or hold a time

You do not need an appointment. Walk-ins are welcome any time between 9:30 and 5:30, Monday to Saturday.

If you would rather have a set time, call 07963 510657 or send a message on Instagram or Facebook.

The salon runs a loyalty card: £5 off a treatment after five visits.

Questions people ask

Do I need to book?

No. Walk-ins are welcome. If you want a set time, book by phone or message.

Where exactly is the salon?

10 Manor Street, Falkirk FK1 1NH, under the pink Perfect Brows & Beauty sign. It is the old Falkirk Herald office.

How long has the salon been open?

Since 2012. The first seven years were in Callendar Square, and the salon has been on Manor Street since November 2020.

Who runs the salon?

Shazia Irfan owns the salon. She is a fully qualified beauty therapist.
