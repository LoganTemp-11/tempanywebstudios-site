Recalibration Sessions: You Don't Have to Start Again to Improve

Concept note: this page shows how a post reads on the new site. It is a short summary. Dannika's full post, in her own words, moves across at launch and is on her current site now.

Recalibration Sessions are one to one, in person sessions for qualified nail technicians. They are not a beginner course and not another qualification. Dannika starts from the skills a tech already has and looks for the small adjustments that change the result.

What a session covers

Safe preparation, product control and application, structure and balance, refining the finished nail, and troubleshooting problems such as lifting and breakages. Each one is shaped around the tech in the chair. The name comes from her engineering background: check how something is performing, then adjust it.

Three hours, and a handbook

In the first session she watched the tech work on a single nail before changing anything, then worked through prep, builder gel, gel polish and finishing. Each tech leaves with a handbook written for them, with before and after photos of their own work.

Pay what you can

There is no minimum fee. She remembers not being able to afford the help she needed, and wants one to one teaching within reach of the techs who need it most. Spaces are limited, in Fife and Central Scotland.
