Common questions

Which tier is my design?

Bronze is simple art in one colour. Silver uses two or more colours. Gold is layered work made to order. Platinum is character art, extreme 3D and extreme lengths. Not sure? Message Dannika when you book and send a photo of the idea.

How does the deposit work?

A £10 booking fee secures every appointment. It is non refundable, but it moves to a future booking if you give 24 hours' notice. After you book, Dannika contacts you within 24 hours with how to pay it.

Can I change the design on the day?

It may not be possible. Appointments run back to back, so the time booked is the time there is. Talk the design through when you book.

Can you fix nails done somewhere else?

Repairs are on the booking page. One Google reviewer had hers repaired at short notice before her wedding.

Where is the desk?

Inside Ghost, the tattoo and piercing studio at 29 Chapel Street, Dunfermline KY12 7AW, in the Heritage Quarter and near public transport.

Who can book?

Appointments are for adults only, and by appointment only.

How do I pay?

Card and contactless are taken at the desk.
