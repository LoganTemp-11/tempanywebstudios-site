Dannika Bernadette, nail artist in Dunfermline since 2017

Dannika began seeing clients in 2017 from a small room in her flat and moved into a salon in 2018. Lockdown stopped everything, then her daughter arrived. She kept seeing clients through most of it.

Moving out of the salon and into a tattoo studio is where, in her words, she became herself and not an extension of someone else. She entered Scratch Stars in 2023, reached the final in 2025 and won Gel Polish Stylist of the Year in 2026.

The winning set told the story of the Moon landings, Apollo on one hand and Artemis on the other, painted from memory in an hour. She calls herself a bit of a space nerd.

Behind the desk she does freehand, detailed nail art and careful structure. Behind the keyboard she writes for other techs.
