Privacy policy

Dogs Galore is run by Violet, Unit 6, 2 Barnpark Drive, Tillicoultry FK13 6BZ. Contact: violetgleed59@gmail.com, 01259 750066.

What we collect

When you use the groom request form, your name, phone number and your dog's details are put into an email on your own device. Nothing is stored by this website. Once you send it, Violet keeps the email to arrange your appointment.

Why

To reply to you and book your dog's groom (legitimate interest and steps before a contract). Notes about your dog, such as coat, temperament and how you like them trimmed, are kept so each groom can follow on from the last.

Analytics

If you allow it, Google Analytics measures which pages are visited. It is off unless you say yes, and you can change your choice any time from "Cookie settings" at the foot of the home page.

How long

Booking emails and dog notes are kept while you are a client and deleted on request. Analytics data is kept for 14 months.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details by emailing Violet. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
