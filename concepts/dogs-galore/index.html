Questions

Before the first groom.

Do you groom large breeds?

Yes. Violet grooms dogs of every size, from small breeds to the big ones. Tick "Large" on the form so she can plan the time.

My puppy has never been groomed. Is that okay?

That's one of the best times to come. A first groom is kept calm and gentle so your puppy gets used to the table, the clippers and the noise.

My dog is nervous or a rescue.

Tell Violet when you book. Reviews mention rescues being put at ease, and she will take it at your dog's pace.

How much does a groom cost?

It depends on the breed, size and condition of the coat. Violet will give you a price when you book.

Can I get in at short notice?

Sometimes. When there's a cancellation Violet will try to fit you in, so it's always worth asking.

Where do I park?

Dogs Galore is at Unit 6, 2 Barnpark Drive, Tillicoultry, with accessible parking at the unit. Please use the Dogs Galore spaces rather than the neighbouring businesses'.
