Privacy policy

Hair at Twenty 8 is run by Sarah Campbell, 28 Pine Crescent, Menstrie FK11 7DT. Contact: sarahlouise2808@mail.com, 07756 563383.

What we collect

When you use the booking form, your name, phone number, chosen service, days and note are written into a WhatsApp message on your own device. Nothing is stored by this website. Once you send it, Sarah holds the message in WhatsApp to arrange your appointment.

Why

To reply to you and book your appointment (legitimate interest and steps before a contract). Client records such as skin test results and colour formulas are kept so your colour can be repeated safely.

Analytics

If you allow it, Google Analytics measures which pages are visited. It is off unless you say yes, and you can change your choice any time from "Cookie settings" at the foot of the home page.

How long

Booking messages are kept while you are a client and deleted on request. Analytics data is kept for 14 months.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details by emailing Sarah. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
