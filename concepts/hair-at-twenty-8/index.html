About Sarah

"You have never been just a client to me."

Hair at Twenty 8 is Sarah's own salon at 28 Pine Crescent in Menstrie. One chair, one colourist, and enough time to get it right, whether that's an hour or four.

She specialises in colour: reds with real depth, soft blondes, balayage and the slow work of taking someone lighter without wrecking their hair. Colour is Wella Professional. Aftercare is L'ANZA healing hair care, because the shade only lasts as long as the hair under it.

Clients come back for the colour and stay for the chat. Sarah remembers where you left off last time.

Good to know

Questions before your first visit

Where is the salon?

28 Pine Crescent, Menstrie, FK11 7DT. A few minutes from Alva, Tillicoultry and the Stirling road.

Do I need a skin test before colour?

New colour clients have a quick skin test at least 48 hours before their appointment. Sarah will arrange it when you book.

How long does a colour appointment take?

Anything from an hour for a root colour to around four hours for a big change or correction. Sarah will tell you when she sees your hair.

Can I see Sarah before I commit?

Yes. Book a consultation, bring a photo of what you love, and she'll tell you honestly what's possible and how many visits it takes.

Do you do wedding hair?

Yes, for brides and bridal parties, with a trial beforehand. Message with your date as early as you can.

What products do you use?

Wella Professional colour and L'ANZA healing hair care, which you can also buy to take home.
