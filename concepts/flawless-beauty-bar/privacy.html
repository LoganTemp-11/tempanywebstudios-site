Privacy notice

Flawless Beauty Bar is a beauty salon in Vosloorus, Gauteng. Contact: flawlessbeautybar26@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you use the booking form, your name, cell number, chosen service, date, referral name and note are written into an email on your own device. This website stores nothing. Once you send it, we keep the email to arrange your appointment.

Why

To reply to you, book your appointment and track referral rewards. We process it under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) because you asked us to book you in.

Analytics

If you allow it, Google Analytics measures which pages are visited. It is off unless you say yes, and you can change your choice any time from "Cookie settings" at the foot of the home page.

How long

Booking emails are kept while you are a client and deleted on request. Analytics data is kept for 14 months.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details by emailing us. You can also complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.
