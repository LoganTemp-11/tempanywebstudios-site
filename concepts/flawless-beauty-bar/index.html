Good to know

Before you book

Is braiding hair included?

Only where the price list says "our hair", "our fibre" or "our bundles". Otherwise bring your own, or ask and we'll tell you what to buy.

How long do knotless braids take?

It depends on length and size. Tell us the style when you book and we'll give you a time so you can plan the day.

Can I bring my own wig to be installed?

Yes. Basic installation is R250 and styled installation is R350. If it needs a wash or customising first, add a wig wash or wig customisation.

What's the difference between cluster lashes and cluster volume?

Cluster volume adds extra fullness to the same look. Natural, cat-eye and wispy all come in both.

How does the referral reward work?

Refer five new clients and your next service of choice is free. Each friend just mentions your name when they book.

Where are you?
