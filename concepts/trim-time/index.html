Questions

Before you come in

Do I need to book?

No. Walk in and take a seat. If you're on a tight schedule, ring ahead and we'll tell you how long the wait is.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes, and we're patient with it. A few of our reviews are from parents and grandparents whose kids won't sit for anyone else.

How much is a haircut?

Prices are on the board in the shop. Customers describe us as very reasonably priced for the quality, and we aim to keep it that way. Call for a number before you come in.

Can I pay by card?

Yes, card and cash both fine.

Where do I park?

The town centre car parks are a couple of minutes' walk from the precinct.
