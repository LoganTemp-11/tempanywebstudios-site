Privacy

This site is run by Trim Time Barbershop, 7 La Porte Precinct, Grangemouth FK3 8AZ. It collects as little as possible.

What we collect

If you use the message form, we receive the name, contact details and message you type. We use them to reply to you and for nothing else. They are sent to us by email through a form service (Web3Forms) and kept in our inbox until the enquiry is dealt with, then deleted within twelve months.

If you call us, we see your number like any phone would. We do not store it unless you ask us to.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies. Visitor numbers are measured with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and does not identify you, so there is nothing to ask your consent for. If that ever changes, a consent notice will appear before anything is set.

Links to other sites

Google Maps and Google reviews are linked, not embedded. Google's own privacy policy applies once you are on their site.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for it corrected or deleted, or complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Ask in the shop or call 07727 934222.
