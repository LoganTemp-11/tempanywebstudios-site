02

What "local search groundwork" means

It's unglamorous. A title that names the trade and the town. A Business Profile carrying exactly the same name, number and address as the site, because a mismatch is what quietly drops you off the map. Structured data so Google reads the business as a business, with Stirling as the area it serves. And a page that opens fast on 4G in Bridge of Allan, not just on the office wifi.

None of that is a ranking promise. Nobody can promise a position on Google. It's the groundwork that most Stirling sites built five years ago never had, done as part of the build rather than sold to you afterwards.
